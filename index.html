Our story

From the first sip of morning to the last toast of the night.

Sip Caffe Bar is a neighbourhood spot in Hipodrom where carefully pulled espresso, creamy matcha and slow mornings on the sofa meet evening spritzes, house cocktails and a good glass of wine.

Warm wood, soft light and a terrace that comes alive after sunset — come for a coffee, stay for a drink.
